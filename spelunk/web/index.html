Welcome to Spelunk

Spelunk scans your Mac to show what's filling your disk. It only reads file names and sizes, everything stays on this Mac, and nothing is deleted or moved unless you confirm it.

Why macOS will ask for permission

macOS protects some folders, such as Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Mail, Messages, Photos and other apps' data. They're often where hidden "System Data" lives, so Spelunk needs your permission to measure them. Choose how you'd like to give it:

Recommended

Allow Full Disk Access

One switch covers everything, so you won't get a separate pop-up for each folder.

  1. Click Open System Settings.
  2. Find Spelunk in the list and switch it on. If it isn't listed, click + and choose Spelunk in Applications.
  3. When macOS asks, click Quit & Reopen.

After switching it on, quit Spelunk and open it again.

Ask me folder by folder

Start scanning now. macOS will ask separately for each protected folder it reaches. Anything you don't allow is shown as "no access", and you can turn on Full Disk Access later.

You can change this any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access.

Your disk

    Data left behind by apps you've deleted, and apps you haven't opened for months. Tick what you don't need, then use the review list. Apps go to the Bin through Finder, which may ask for your password.

    Identical files in your own folders (Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Movies…). Apps, photo and music libraries, Library and project folders like node_modules are never compared.

    Copies made with Finder on APFS can share disk space. Removing one of those frees less than shown.
